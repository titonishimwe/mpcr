Implementation Framework

Strategies & Target Beneficiaries

How MPCR drives sustainable grassroots mobilization and lasting community ownership.

Core Delivery Strategies

  • Christian Cinema & Road Shows: Mobile open-air screenings conveying gospel truths and public health messages.
  • Evangelization Campaigns: Intensive community preaching, revival seminars, and youth fellowship rallies.
  • Lobby & Advocacy: Defending the rights of women, child protection, and land restoration compliance.
  • Capacity Building: Training Child Protection Committees (CPCs), gender counselors, and farmer facilitators.
  • Decentralized Nurseries: Producing quality agroforestry and fruit seedlings directly within intervention sectors.

Priority Target Beneficiaries

  • Churches & Congregations: Equipping pastors, Sunday school teachers, and faith youth leaders.
  • Children & University Students: Fostering child protection, scholarships, and Christian values.
  • Women & Widows: Income-generating cooperatives, livestock incentives, and cookstoves.
  • Prisoners, Refugees & Informal Couples: Pastoral reconciliation, counseling, and social reintegration.
  • Smallholder Farmers & Cooperatives: Training champion farmers in tree maintenance, soil care, and agroforestry.
Prospect

By the End of 2026

MPCR will become a major participant in Rwanda in accomplishing its mission and achieving its objectives. By the end of 2026 it will have carried out the following work.

Youth, students, and child rights

  • Mobilizing 20,000 students of all universities in Rwanda for self-reliance, leadership development, child rights, and improved living conditions, based on Christianity and evangelization.
  • Training 25,000 Rwandan youth on child rights.
  • Creating Child Protection Committees and grouping vulnerable groups into cooperatives.
  • Training beneficiaries on violence and how to conduct advocacy and lobby.
  • Combating school dropout within a human rights framework.
  • Promoting community debate within a human rights framework.

Livelihoods and household support

  • Supporting 50 vulnerable cooperatives for poverty reduction and food balance.
  • Promoting community financial saving in rural areas (Ibimina).
  • Women Economic Empowerment Project.
  • Youth Skills and Employment Project.
  • Vulnerable Household Graduation Project.
  • Community Savings and Entrepreneurship Project.
  • Agricultural Livelihoods and Food Security Project.
  • Green and Social Enterprise Development Project.

Health, climate, and community well-being

  • Combating HIV among youth and in all communities.
  • Combating disease in all communities, especially among youth.
  • Combating climate change.
  • Combating malnutrition in children.

Education, media, and headquarters

  • Promoting Sunday school centers.
  • Creating a secondary school.
  • Creating a university institution.
  • Creating community radio and television.
  • Building our headquarters.

Church, leadership, and ministry

  • Training pastors and church leaders.
  • Supporting Bible schools and theological institutions.
  • Developing Christian leadership and discipleship programs.
  • Providing teaching materials and resources for churches.
  • Partnering with Baptist conventions and ministries around the world.
  • Strengthening local churches through biblical education.
  • Pastor and church leader training.
  • Bible and theological education.
  • Church planting initiatives.
  • Christian leadership development.
  • Ministry resource development.
