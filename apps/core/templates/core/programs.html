Implementation Framework

Strategies & Target Beneficiaries

How MPCR drives sustainable grassroots mobilization and lasting community ownership.

Core Delivery Strategies

  • Christian Cinema & Road Shows: Mobile open-air screenings conveying gospel truths and public health messages.
  • Evangelization Campaigns: Intensive community preaching, revival seminars, and youth fellowship rallies.
  • Lobby & Advocacy: Defending the rights of women, child protection, and land restoration compliance.
  • Capacity Building: Training Child Protection Committees (CPCs), gender counselors, and farmer facilitators.
  • Decentralized Nurseries: Producing quality agroforestry and fruit seedlings directly within intervention sectors.

Priority Target Beneficiaries

  • Churches & Congregations: Equipping pastors, Sunday school teachers, and faith youth leaders.
  • Children & University Students: Fostering child protection, scholarships, and Christian values.
  • Women & Widows: Income-generating cooperatives, livestock incentives, and cookstoves.
  • Prisoners, Refugees & Informal Couples: Pastoral reconciliation, counseling, and social reintegration.
  • Smallholder Farmers & Cooperatives: Training champion farmers in tree maintenance, soil care, and agroforestry.
