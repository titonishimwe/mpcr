Who We Are

Guided by Christian Faith, Dedicated to People and Planet

Founded in Kinyinya, Gasabo District, MPCR began with a divine vision to unite evangelism with concrete community development. We believe that true spiritual transformation flourishes when human dignity, food security, environmental stewardship, and physical health are restored together.

Our field interventions span Nyarugenge, Gasabo, and Kicukiro in Kigali City; Gatsibo in the Eastern Province; Rusizi, Ngororero, and Rutsiro in the Western Province; and Nyanza and Kamonyi in the Southern Province.

Our Vision

Leading positive change by preaching the Gospel, promoting good living, economic resilience, and environmental sustainability.

Our Mission

To make Christ known and transform lives through evangelism, community-led initiatives, and socio-economic empowerment.
