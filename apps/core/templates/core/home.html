Organizational Profile

Who We Are

Mouvement Pour Christ au Rwanda (MPCR) is a faith-based non-governmental organization working in Rwanda. It was created out of a deep Christian conviction that faith must be demonstrated through practical action and holistic human upliftment.

Our approach integrates the message of Christ with sustainable socio-economic development. We walk alongside rural and urban communities to strengthen family stability, safeguard vulnerable children, equip youth with income-generating skills, empower women through cooperatives, protect the natural environment, and foster community health.
