Our History & Identity

About MPCR

Mouvement Pour Christ au Rwanda (Movement for Christ in Rwanda) is a religious non-governmental organization dedicated to preaching the Gospel and advancing socio-economic transformation.

Historical Background

Rooted in Kigali, Reaching Across the Nation

MPCR started its grassroots mission at Kinyinya in Gasabo District, Kigali City. Conceived as a response to both the spiritual thirst and the practical socio-economic vulnerabilities of rural and peri-urban populations, the organization has grown into an impactful nationwide institution.

The organization is governed by its official statutes and is fully subject to the provisions of Law No. 06/2012 of 17/02/2012 governing Civil Society Organizations and Faith-Based Organizations (FBOs) in the Republic of Rwanda. MPCR was granted its official Legal Personality Certificate of Compliance No. 166/2023 by the Rwanda Governance Board (RGB).

Head Office Location

The MPCR national head office is situated at Kigali City, Nyarugenge District, Nyamirambo Sector, providing strategic administrative coordination for all field programs and regional initiatives.

Pillars of MPCR

Vision, Mission & Guiding Identity

Anchored in our guiding principle: "Roho nzima itura mu mubiri muzima" (A healthy spirit lives in a healthy body).

Our Vision

Being a Faith Christian Organization leading positive change by preaching the gospel, promoting good living, economic development, and sustainable development because "A healthy spirit lives in a healthy body."

Our Mission

To make Christ known and transform the lives of the people through evangelism, activities that improve the lives of the people, increase economic capacity, and promote sustainable development.

Our Core Values (Indangagaciro)

Gukunda Imana no Kuyubaha

Devoted love and reverent fear of God guiding every decision, stewardship practice, and interpersonal interaction.

Urukundo n'Impuhwe

Unconditional Christian love and compassion, reaching out especially to the most vulnerable, widows, orphans, and prisoners.

Ubunyangamugayo

Uncompromising honesty, moral uprightness, and trustworthy financial and institutional stewardship.

Gukorera mu Mucyo

Transparency and open accountability toward beneficiaries, government authorities, donor partners, and churches.

Ubumwe n'Ubufatanye

Unity, mutual respect, teamwork, and strong collaborative alliances across communities and civil society organizations.

Iterambere Rirambye

Sustainable development integrating environmental restoration with economic resilience for enduring community prosperity.

Leadership & Administration

Organs & Governance Structure

MPCR maintains a clear, accountable, and transparent administrative structure to ensure rigorous oversight.

1. General Assembly

The supreme decision-making and policy formulation body of MPCR, representing general membership and organizational vision.

2. Executive Committee

Responsible for strategic leadership, operational oversight, program approvals, and performance agreements.

3. Audit Council

Independent internal audit and compliance organ ensuring fiscal discipline, regulatory adherence, and integrity.

4. Executive Secretariat

Headed by the Legal Representative, directing daily program executions across 10 specialized functional departments.
